**See what a character means in Chinese.** The Regional forms view now lists the first meanings English Wiktionary gives a character in Chinese, so you can see where a meaning has moved: 湯 is "broth; soup" in Chinese, and "hot or boiling water" is marked literary. The notes from Wiktionary now show their lists as lists, and fold the history of the Chinese word under **More from Wiktionary**. Select a form in the chart to copy it. Readings start with Japan's, and Cantonese tone numbers are raised and explained. On a kanji's page, **Regional forms** is now the third link, after **Stroke order** and **Component tree**.
